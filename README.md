# 🔗 MATSYNC AI
AI-powered platform for standardizing, matching and harmonizing material codes across CPSEs with engineering-aware validation.

### AI-Powered Material Standardization & Harmonization Platform

MATSYNC AI helps CPSEs identify and harmonize materials that may have different codes, descriptions, or technical specifications.
It combines AI-based matching with technical validation to identify equivalent materials while preventing unsafe or incorrect merges.

---

# 🎯 Problem

Different CPSEs may represent the same material using different:

- Material codes
- Descriptions
- Units
- Classifications
- Technical specifications

This can lead to duplicate records, inconsistent data, fragmented procurement, and difficulty finding equivalent materials.

---

# 💡 Solution

MATSYNC AI follows a simple workflow:

📥 **CPSE Data**  
            ↓  
🧹 **Normalize**  
            ↓  
🧬 **Extract Material DNA**  
            ↓  
🤖 **AI Matching**  
            ↓  
⚙️ **Technical Conflict Check**  
            ↓  
👨‍💻 **Human Validation**  
            ↓  
🆔 **National Material Identity**
